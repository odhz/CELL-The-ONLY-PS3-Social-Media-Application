# CELL Features

**CELL: The ONLY PS3 Social Media Application.** Everything CELL can do as of version 1.1.6.

## Your account
- **Claim a unique username.** Only one person can have each name. You can also show your PSN ID on your profile, or keep it hidden.
- **Change your username** in Settings once every 30 days (changing only capitals is always free). Names with racism, slurs or hate aren't allowed.
- **No sign-in hassle.** Pick a name once and CELL remembers you.
- **Your account survives a reinstall.** It's backed up on your PS3 and linked to your console, so deleting CELL, or even swapping the hard drive, doesn't lose it.
- **Lost access? Get it back.** CELL staff can give you a one-time recovery code: choose "I already have an account" and type it in.
- **Automatic updates.** CELL tells you when a new version is out, then downloads and installs it by itself and restarts on the new version.

## Profiles
- **Profile pictures:** choose a ready-made one, use your own photo, or use an **animated GIF** that plays everywhere your picture shows.
- **Banners, bios and "Now playing"** to show what you're into.
- **Your game library** on your profile, so people can see what you play.
- **Badges:** Owner, Admin, Moderator, Donator, Promoter, Beta-Tester, Creator, PS3 Developer, Bug Hunter and CELL Gamer, each with its own icon, shown from the most important on the left. If you have more than one, pick which shows next to your name.
- **Console tag:** profiles show which console a player is on.
- **Your wall:** friends can post on it, and you choose who's allowed: everyone, friends only or nobody.
- **Stats:** GGs received, posts, followers, following, friends and games, plus Arcade wins, losses and streaks.
- **Socials:** show your YouTube, Twitch, X, Instagram, TikTok, Bluesky, Reddit, Kick and Discord. Others scan a QR code to open them on their phone. You choose who sees them.

## Feed
- **Post text, screenshots, GIFs and videos.** Videos up to 60 seconds come from your PS3's Video folder, a USB stick, or your phone by scanning a QR code, and play with sound right on the PS3.
- **Four feeds:** Everyone, Following, My games and Mentions. Switch with L1/R1.
- **GG** (like) and **comment** on posts, and **@mention** other players.
- **GG comments** and **reply** to them with an emoji or a GIF. Replies show the comment they answer.
- **1,384 emoji** in nine tabs (Popular, Faces, People, Animals, Food, Travel, Activities, Objects, Symbols), each with a shortcode you can type, like `:fire:` or `:pizza:`.
- **GIF search** built in, with six **CELL GIFs** always first (GG, Welcome to CELL, Let's play!, Online tonight?, LOL and the CELL logo).
- **Use your own pictures and GIFs** from `/dev_hdd0/photo` (send them over FTP) or a USB drive.
- **View pictures full screen** with R3.

## Messages
- **Private messages** with text, emoji, GIFs and pictures.
- **Message requests:** friends go straight to your inbox and everyone else waits in Requests until you accept, so you decide who you talk to.
- **"Is typing..."** and **Delivered / Seen** indicators.
- **Message anyone** from their profile, your Friends list or any post.

## Global Chat
- **One chat room for everyone on CELL,** with GIFs, pictures and emoji.
- **Reply** to any message: your reply shows the message it answers, and they get a notification.
- **See who's chatting** and who's typing.
- **A badge on the side menu** counts new messages since you last looked (up to 99+).

## Play Online: revived PS3 games
- **90+ PS3 games** that fan groups have brought back online, including PS Rewired, PSONE, Warhawk Revived, PS Reborn and more.
- **Step-by-step DNS setup** for every game.
- **Live status** for each game: Online again, Official, In testing, Ending or Ended, so you know what still works.
- **"It worked / It's broken" reports** from other players over the last 7 days.
- **Who on CELL owns each game,** so you can find people to play with.
- **Game nights:** plan one, RSVP, and get a reminder an hour before.
- **Alerts:** follow a game that's still in testing and get notified when it goes live.
- **Search and filter** by game, group or status.

## The Arcade
- **Five games against other players:** Tic-Tac-Toe, Checkers, Pong, Cell Division (up to 10 players) and Reaction Duel.
- **Find a match, challenge a friend** (they get a pop-up), or **practise against CELL**.
- **Wins, losses and streaks** on your profile, the week's top players, and the **CELL Gamer** badge at 50 wins (open a ticket in the CELL Discord to get it).

## Games
- **Build your library** by scanning your PS3 or searching the catalog. Box art loads automatically.
- **See how many players** on CELL own each game.

## Friends and finding players
- **Friends and followers**, with an online / offline list on the Feed. Everyone follows CELL's creator, Dehz, so CELL news reaches every player.
- **See how many players are on CELL** right on the Feed.
- **Find players** by name.
- **Notifications** for GGs, comments, mentions, friend requests, wall posts and messages.

## News
- **PS3 homebrew, revival and scene news** from around the web, in one place.
- **Read full articles right on your PS3,** with ads and clutter stripped out.
- **A Revivals tab** for online-revival news.
- **Scan to read:** every story has a QR code to open the full article on your phone.
- **PS3 and PS Vita stories from Brewology.**

## Safety
- **Block players.** It works both ways: you won't see each other anywhere on CELL, and they aren't told.
- **Report** posts, comments, messages and players.
- **Active moderation** with a word filter in 7 languages that sees through disguised spellings, mutes and bans. New videos can be checked by staff before they show.
- **Public bans:** a red bar on a banned player's profile shows how long they're banned and why. Banned players show as offline.
- **Clear rules,** shown in the app.
- **Send feedback or an idea** to the CELL team straight from Settings.

## Talk to everyone
- **Clear warnings:** a warning from the moderators fills the screen in red, stays up 5 seconds, and closes only after you tick that you've read it.
- **Auto-translate:** posts, comments, Global Chat and private messages show in your language, with the original one button away. It catches short messages and chat slang too, and reads Polish, Czech, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian and more. PS3s set to Dutch, Polish, Russian, Turkish, Swedish, Danish, Finnish or Norwegian get posts in that language.

## Accessibility
- **Text size:** Normal, Large or Extra large for posts, comments, chat, messages and news.
- **High contrast** and **colour-blind friendly** colours.
- **Reduce motion:** still backgrounds, no sparkles, GIFs stay still.
- **Read aloud:** CELL speaks what you select through your TV, in your language.
- **Picture descriptions** on screenshots, for players who can't see them.
- **Alerts you can see and feel:** a screen flash and controller rumble for new messages, and pop-ups that stay as long as you need.

## Sound
- **Background music:** 5 calm tracks made for CELL, shuffled and fading smoothly into each other.
- **Menu sounds:** soft clicks as you move around.
- Each has its own on/off switch in Settings, and the music dips while Read aloud is talking.

## Made for the PS3
- **8 languages:** English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian and Arabic (joined letters, right to left). CELL follows your PS3's language, or pick one in Settings.
- **Predictive text:** the PS3 keyboard opens in your language with word suggestions.
- **Time & weather** at the top of the Feed, from your connection or a city you pick. Your city is never shown to other players or staff, and you can turn it off.
- **Full controller navigation,** with button hints on every screen.
- **USB mouse and keyboard:** point and click anything, scroll with the wheel (the page never scrolls by itself), or use the arrow keys and Enter.
- **30 accent colours and animated backgrounds,** including RGB flow, XMB waves, Starfield, Synthwave and Snowfall.
- **Fast pictures:** images are saved on your hard drive, so they load instantly next time.
- **Join the official Discord** by scanning a QR code with your phone.
- **Link your Discord** to get the CELL Player role in the CELL Discord.
- **Check connection:** if CELL won't connect, it shows which part isn't working and what to do. If your DNS can't find CELL, it finds it another way.
- **A fresh look on the XMB:** a transparent CELL thumbnail, and a background that changes every time you start CELL.
- **Works on CFW and PS3HEN.**
