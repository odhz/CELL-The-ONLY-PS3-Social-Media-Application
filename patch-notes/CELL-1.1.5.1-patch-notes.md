# CELL 1.1.5 Hotfix 1 (1.1.5.1) Patch Notes: music, more languages, change your username, CELL GIFs and comment GGs

## New

- **Background music and menu sounds.** A calm, quiet music loop made for CELL, and soft clicks as you move around the menus. Turn either one off in **Settings → Background music** and **Settings → Menu sounds**. The music gets quieter while Read aloud is talking.
- **Global Chat badge.** The side menu shows how many new Global Chat messages there are since you last looked, up to **99+**. Opening Global Chat clears it.
- **More languages.**
  - Auto-translate now catches far more posts, comments, chat and messages, including short ones and slang ("Salve", "jugamos minecraft", "vc joga?").
  - CELL can now show Polish, Czech, Turkish, Romanian, Hungarian, Croatian, Russian and Ukrainian letters. They used to show as "?".
  - PS3s set to Dutch, Polish, Russian, Turkish, Swedish, Danish, Finnish or Norwegian get posts translated into that language (the menus stay in English for now).
  - Posts in Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Greek and other alphabets the PS3 can't show are translated into your language.
- **Change your username.** New in **Settings → Username** (the top row). Once every 30 days; changing only capitals is always free.
  - Names with racism, slurs or hate aren't allowed, and CELL staff are told when someone tries one. Swearing is fine.
  - Your old name stays in your account history, so staff can always see who you were.
- **CELL GIFs.** Six animated CELL GIFs are built in and always come first in the GIF picker: **GG**, **Welcome to CELL**, **Let's play!**, **Online tonight?**, **LOL** and the **CELL** logo. Use them on posts, comments, Global Chat and messages. They work even when GIF search is off.
- **Apply to be a moderator.** New in **Settings → Apply to be a moderator**. Dehz reads every application.
  - Link your Discord first (Settings → Link Discord), so Dehz can reach you.
  - Three parts: **About you**, **Your experience**, and **What would you do on CELL?** (eight real situations, from fights in Global Chat to a player who seems unsafe).
  - **The more detail you give, the better.** Short answers get a reminder before sending.
  - **Proof of moderating:** add up to 4 screenshots (from the PS3 or a USB stick) and 3 links. Only Dehz sees them.
  - Tick that you've read the CELL rules, then press △ to send. One application at a time, and once every 30 days.
- **Accepted players get a welcome screen.** "You're a CELL moderator!" shows the orange Moderator badge and their Staff site login, shown only once and wiped from the server when they close it.
- **GG comments.** On a post, press the D-pad down to pick a comment: □ gives it a GG (press again to take it back), ✕ replies to it with their name filled in, then add an emoji (△) or a GIF (□) before sending. The commenter gets a notification.
- **Hotfixes.** Small fixes between releases now come as hotfixes, like this one (1.1.5.1, shown as "1.1.5 Hotfix 1"). Your PS3 asks you to update the same way as a normal release.

## Improved

- **Comments are easier to get around.** Posts now show **UP/DOWN Comments** in the button guide, and after you send a comment or reply, the list scrolls to it.
- **Comments stay inside their box.** On posts with lots of comments, scrolling no longer shows comments above the comments panel.
- **Fixed: the comment box going blank** after replying on a post with lots of comments (and GIFs). It no longer needs backing out and reopening.
- **New sign-ups** are checked for racism, slurs and hate too, including disguised spellings (N1gg3r, n_i_g_g_a, H3il).
- **Temporary staff passwords are easier to copy off a TV:** no more look-alike letters such as 0 and O, or 1, l and I.

## For staff

- **Username changes.** If a player tries a name with racism, slurs or hate, it's refused and a **Word filter** report on that player appears in Reports with the name they tried (one an hour at most). Every change shows in their History and name history.
- **Storage & clean-up (owner only, Updates page).** Clear Global Chat (older than 1, 7 or 30 days, or all of it) and remove private conversations nobody has written in for 30 to 365 days. Anything with an open report is kept. Keeps the database small and CELL quick. When Global Chat is cleared, a message from Dehz tells everyone in chat, for example "Dehz has cleared chat older than 7 days for housekeeping…".
- **Server updates no longer show an error page.** While an update installs, the Staff site says "CELL's server is restarting", reconnects by itself and reloads into the new version when it's done. The web front (Caddy) is also no longer restarted during an update unless its settings changed.
- **Applications page (owner only).** Waiting, Accepted and Declined tabs.
  - Each application shows the answers next to the player's record: days on CELL, posts and GGs, any warnings, mutes or bans, how their reports turned out, how long the answers are and how long they took.
  - Proof photos open full size; links open in a new tab.
  - **Accept** gives the Moderator badge, makes a **moderator** Staff site login linked to their player account and sends it privately to their PS3. Everyone starts as a moderator: only the owner makes admins, on the Staff page.
  - **Decline** with an optional reason (the player is told, and can apply again in 30 days). Add private notes to any application.
  - Phone alerts can tell you when someone applies (Phone alerts → "Moderator applications").
- **Staff page:**
  - **Rename** a staff login (their password and history stay the same).
  - **Reset password** can now send the new password privately to their PS3, shown once in CELL, instead of you passing it on.
- **Discord:** a new **Moderator role** field. Players you accept from an application get that role automatically.
- **Updates page:** a **Hotfix** button fills in the next hotfix number (1.1.5 → 1.1.5.1 → 1.1.5.2) and ticks "Players must update".
